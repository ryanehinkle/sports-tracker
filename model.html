Historical consistency blends L5, L10, head-to-head, current season and prior season rates. Small samples are shrunk toward a neutral prior.

Usage stability measures each player's share of team receiving targets, rushing attempts, and total opportunities.

Opponent DvP rebuilds QB/RB/WR/TE production allowed by each defense and converts the relevant matchup to a league percentile.

Price efficiency compares estimated probability with sportsbook implied probability. Positive separation helps the score but does not guarantee value.
