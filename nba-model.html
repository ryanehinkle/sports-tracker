NBA model methodology+

NBA-only scoring blends game-log hit rates, recent per-game averages versus the offered line, minutes and shot opportunities, opponent defensive environment, and sportsbook implied probabilities. Models are separate from NFL. Recommendations are estimates, not guaranteed outcomes.

No pregame history is backfilled using later stats. Dates without a pregame NBA snapshot are marked unavailable.
